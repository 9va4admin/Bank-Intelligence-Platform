A
Login
1
Open browser → go to http://<server-ip> (ask IT for the address)
2
Enter your username (bank email) and password
3
Enter the 6-digit OTP from your authenticator app (or SMS)
4
You are now on the Dashboard. Your role and branch appear top-right
Session expires after 15 min of inactivity. You will be logged out automatically.
B
Start Scanner Session
1
Ensure the Canon CR-120 is powered on and the green LED is steady
2
Navigate to CTS → Outward → New Session
3
Enter the Session ID (given by your supervisor) and click Start Session
4
Feed cheques face-up. The system displays each cheque's MICR line and status in real time
5
When all cheques are scanned, click End Session. Do not power off the scanner first
C
Inward Queue — Review & Decision
1
Go to CTS → Inward → Review Queue. Cheques requiring human review appear here (AI confidence below threshold)
2
Click a cheque row to open it. Review: front & rear images, MICR line, AI fraud score, SHAP explanation, stop-payment status
3
Select your decision below and add a reason (required for non-STP decisions)
Approve (STP)
Cheque passes all checks. Click Approve. Filed to NGCH automatically. Cannot be reversed once filed.
Return
Select a Return Reason from the list (RBI-standard codes). Instrument returned to presenting bank via NGCH.
Escalate
Refer to Branch Manager or Fraud Analyst. Escalate before the IET countdown reaches 0.
IET Timer — the countdown in red next to each cheque is the RBI-mandated decision deadline. If it reaches zero, the system auto-files. Never leave a cheque unreviewed when the timer is under 5 minutes.
D
Escalation Procedure
1
Open the cheque → click Escalate
2
Select recipient: Branch Manager (fraud / high value) or Fraud Analyst (AI uncertainty)
3
Type escalation note (minimum 20 characters). Click Send Escalation
4
The cheque moves to the recipient's queue. You are notified by WhatsApp when a decision is made
5
If the IET countdown is under 10 minutes, call the Branch Manager directly — do not rely on notifications alone
E
Common Issues
Scanner not detecting chequeCheck green LED steady; restart scanner; re-seat USB cable; call IT if persists
MICR read error on chequeRe-feed the cheque once. If fails again, mark as MICR Fail and escalate
"Session already active"Another terminal has an open session. Click View Active Sessions and confirm with supervisor
Login OTP not receivedWait 60 s, then click Resend OTP. Still failing → call IT Helpdesk
Cheque queue is emptyNormal — no cheques in your zone for this session. Check with clearing supervisor
"Vault miss — human review"Signature not on record. Escalate to Branch Manager for manual signature verification
