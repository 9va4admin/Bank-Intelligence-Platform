Read this before the numbers

  • Accuracy figures are against my by-eye readings of all 70 images, which can themselves be wrong (uncertain ones are marked). Indic-script payees are not scored for exactness.
  • Reference data are stand-ins. Lot 3: the presented cheque number and reference payee are my by-eye readings (standing in for NGCH presentment / PPS data). Lots 1 and 2: reference number, amount and payee are also my by-eye readings (payee left UNKNOWN where Indic-script or uncertain). So the cross-check results show the gates working; they are not production accuracy. The MICR branch-master check is switched off for this run (a real master needs the bank's own branch list; the one built from Lot 3 would trivially reject every Lot 1 and 2 instrument), so all three lots are judged by the same remaining gates.
  • Stubbed / not production: CBS and NGCH are dev stubs; the CBS holder names are fixture entries I seeded from the vision read; signatures are each cheque's own image enrolled as the specimen (plumbing, not authenticity); fraud scores come from a real XGBoost + SHAP model that is a synthetic stand-in (not real fraud detection); OCR runs on the cloud model.
  • No instrument was confirmed automatically because the bank ramp mode is FULL_MANUAL: 5 instruments cleared every gate (OCR, payee, signature, fraud, CBS, PPS) and were held for a human only because of that mode, 22 returned on the 90-day stale-date rule, and the rest were sent to review by a gate. Turning on a supervised STP mode would let the 5 clean ones through.
  • Wrong auto-returns found in the first combined run and fixed before this one: two Lot 1 cheques had a 2026 date read as 2096 and 2006 and were auto-returned as post-dated and 7338-days stale; a Lot 2 cheque with a visible Gujarati-script signature was reported as unsigned and auto-returned. A date decades from today is now sent to review (probable OCR misread), and a missing signature goes to review unless the bank opts in to auto-return. This run has those fixes, plus a second look before reporting a signature absent (the detector is model-based and flipped between runs) and an amount-vs-presented cross-check (a Rs 2,00,000 cheque had been read as Rs 9,00,000 in both figures and words).
  • An unreadable payee or amount-in-words is human review, not a pass. Handwritten payees are read twice by two different models and withheld if they disagree.
